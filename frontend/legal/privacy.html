Privacy Policy

This draft policy explains what PayShare stores about you and why.

1. What we collect

  • Your first name, last name and email address.
  • A salted hash of your password and, for contractors, of your payout PIN.
  • Your public Stellar address, if you add one.
  • Project records: budgets, tasks, submissions, approvals, payments and invitations.
  • Your preferences, the date you accepted these drafts, and sign-in sessions.

2. Why we use it

To run your account, show each person the records their role allows, check payment conditions, and email you a verification code.

3. Cookies and device storage

PayShare uses one sign-in cookie. Your browser also keeps your theme choice and, while you are joining a project, the invitation link. There are no advertising or analytics trackers.

4. Who sees it

People on the same project see the records their role allows. Workers do not see other workers’ pay.

When configured, an email provider (Resend) receives your address and the verification message. Stellar testnet transfers, including public addresses and amounts, are visible on a public ledger. An optional AI summary, when enabled by the operator, sends project status information to an AI provider.

5. Keeping and deleting data

Records are kept while the project and account are in use. Self-service deletion is not available yet; ask the team to remove your account. Public blockchain records cannot be deleted.

6. Your choices and contact

You can correct your name and wallet in Settings and change notification preferences there. For access or deletion requests, contact the PayShare team through the project repository or the person who invited you.
