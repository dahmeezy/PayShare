Terms and Conditions

These draft terms describe how PayShare works today and what we expect of the people who use it.

1. What PayShare does

PayShare records project budgets, tasks, agreed pay and approvals, and can release payments in USDC on the Stellar testnet. Testnet assets have no real-world value. In simulation projects no funds move.

2. Your account

You must give your real name and an email address you control, and keep your password and payout PIN private. You are responsible for activity in your account.

Your role (client, contractor or worker) decides what you can see and do. An invitation sets the role for the person it is addressed to.

3. Agreements between users

Budgets, tasks and payment terms are agreements between the people on a project. PayShare records them and is not a party to them, an employer, an escrow agent, a bank or a payment institution.

4. Payments

A payout is released only when the recorded conditions are met and the contractor authorizes it with their PIN. Blockchain transfers cannot be reversed by PayShare. Check wallet addresses carefully. PayShare never asks for a Stellar secret key.

5. Acceptable use

Do not use PayShare to break the law, mislead other people, access records that are not yours, or disrupt the service.

6. Availability and liability

This is an early version provided as it is, without a promise of uninterrupted service. To the extent the law allows, the team is not liable for indirect loss. Nothing here limits rights that cannot be limited by law.

7. Changes and contact

The team may update these terms and will ask you to agree again when a change is material. Contact the PayShare team through the project repository or the person who invited you.
